GOOD QUESTION / 07

LET'S CLEAR
THAT UP.

New to the gym? Totally fine. Here's what people usually want to know before their first session.

I'm a beginner. Can I still join? +

Absolutely. Start with a free trial session and tell the coach about your experience and goals. We'll help you get comfortable with the basics.

What should I bring for my first workout? +

Comfortable workout clothes, clean training shoes, a water bottle, and a towel. Arrive a few minutes early so we can show you around.

Do you offer personal training? +

Yes, personal coaching can be arranged. Use the trial form and select your interest so the team can discuss options with you.

Can I try the gym before choosing a plan? +

That's what the free trial is for. Submit the form and the team can follow up to confirm a suitable time.
